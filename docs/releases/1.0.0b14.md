# Aura 1.0.0b14

Released from `main` at 3b835c25 (#410). Changes since 1.0.0b13.1 (5dcf62d0), whose fix (#213)
`main` carries as well. This release exists for its Stage 1: a 1.0.0b13 or 1.0.0b13.1 Stage 1
cannot start any Stage 2 on the MacBook Air M5 (T8142), and a newer Stage 2 cannot help, because
Stage 1 stops first.

## Stage 1

- **MacBook Air M5 (T8142) ANS handoff (#250, #255).** Before it starts the next stage, a post-M4 Stage 1
  powers ANS off. A 1.0.0b13 Stage 1 does that through the APCIe controller, which it does not
  know on T8142. It logs `pcie: Unsupported compatible` and `nvme: couldn't power ANS off`, refuses
  the NVMe shutdown, finds no valid payload and stops in its proxy. T8142 now powers ANS off and on
  through its PMGR storage gates, as macOS does. The Stage 2 that follows starts it with
  `nvme.sart=`. A T8142 session that an older stage left behind is adopted, or restarted through
  the same gates. An unsupported PCIe controller is now logged with its compatibles.
- **ANS across the handoff on the M4, M4 Pro and M4 Max (#305, #410).**
  - **T6040 and T6041.** Their ANS firmware doesn't start again after a power-off. So a stage
    hands the session on running, with RTKit untouched and the I/O queues cleared, and the next
    aura stage adopts it. Only a session that came running goes on live: iBoot's, or one an earlier
    stage handed on live. A session this stage started from off, after a 1.0.0b11 to 1.0.0b13.1
    Stage 1 powered ANS off, goes on powered off with `nvme.sart=`, as it came.
  - **T8132 always hands ANS on powered off** with `nvme.sart=`, as every 1.0.0b13 stage does. A
    next aura stage's PCIe init takes a synchronous external abort on a live T8132 ANS, whether it
    adopts the session or starts it again. A b14 Stage 1 behind a b13 Stage 1 reset the J713 this
    way at its handoff.
  - Once a session has been handed on live, no SART entries are passed for it, so the next stage
    isn't told that a running ANS is off.
- **MCC (#320, #384, #392).** A known memory controller that fails to initialise, or a carveout
  that fails to unmap, is now fatal on every chip, not only on T8140 and T6040. A SoC whose ADT
  does not describe the MCC is left alone with a log line. The ADT counts are read at 4 or 8
  bytes. On the M4 (T8132), where every ADT stores them in 4 bytes, MCC setup runs for the first
  time (`MCC: Initialized T8132 MCCs`), and the TZ carveouts come from the ADT list.
- **iBoot's failed-boot count (#321).** Stage 1 clears the count in the primary PMU's legacy
  scratchpad (`info-leg_scrpad` + 2) on every chip, as it did on T6040, T8140 and T6050. A hung
  test kernel or a proxy session that ends in a reset no longer adds up to a recoveryOS boot. A
  chip whose ADT has no such PMU node is left alone.
- **CPUs (#229, #312, #316, #317, #337).** Every M4- and M5-generation core takes AIC IRQs. A
  locked RVBAR is never written; writing it boot-looped T8122. A CPU that fails to start, or that
  fails mitigation or TSO setup, refuses the payload handoff on every chip. Secondaries stay off
  the console in their reset path.
- **Execute window (#280, #327).** The chips that need the CTXR execute window are listed in one
  file and checked at boot. An armed CTXR_D is read before a secondary is probed.
- **USB, watchdog and NVMe (#322, #325, #326, #336).** The CDC flush is bounded by drain progress on
  every chip. USB falls back to unnumbered port nodes. The proxy watchdog request works on any chip
  with the v2 watchdog layout. NVMe sets CC.MPS to 4 KiB pages for its PRPs and reports any
  fallback to single-block reads.
- **CPU frequency (#319).** T8140, T6040, T6041 and T8152 share one cluster path.

## Stage 2 and the Linux handoff

- **Displays.** T8132 external displays are handed over by default (#299). The T6041 internal DCP
  and dcpext on macOS 27.2 (#265), dcpext3 firmware memory (#212), the M5 Ultra Mac Studio displays
  (#264), the K116s panel on 27.2 (#226), the J873g's spare DCPEXT0/1 (#300) and the display
  ADMACs' LLT detector tunables (#207) are handed over too. An unresolvable display device is
  refused on every chip (#330).
- **GPU.** T8122 handoff from a three-segment ADT on 27.2 (#393, #396). The M5 Ultra GPU (#261). The
  G17C leakage calibration words (#279). The firmware release is reported to the T6050 GPU driver
  (#230). 27.2 T6041 GPU framing (#373).
- **Firmware preloads and ISP.** The T8142 encoder (#215) and ANE (#251), the M4 ANE (#260), the
  J713 ISP window and address filters (#309, #397), ISP17a selection by compatible (#329), and
  property-driven ISP and DAPF handoff (#304).
- **Other handoffs.** T8142 APCIe and radio-port tables for the sleep controller (#272), AOP motion
  sensor calibration (#306), T6040 USB2 device tunables (#340), the hyphenated `usb4-N-pcie`
  alias (#302), zero-padded ACIO tunables (#254), and SEPFW in DRAM checked on every chip (#331).
  Each Mac keeps a device tree margin (#332). Radio handling and the speaker safety profile are
  chosen by the ADT, not by T8140 (#314, #328).
- **M5 Pro and Max (T6050).** SIO tables and sleep-controller wake sources from the ADT (#214,
  #323). J714c USB through the generic gadget path, the PMP DART and the G17C handoff (#193). The
  K116s camera and G17S firmware on 27.2 (#187, #188), and the K114c ISP (#206). PCIe auto-PM
  storage domains (#203).
- **M5 Ultra (T6050, two dies).** All 30 CPUs (#262). Die-1 power words (#263).

## Upgrading

The installer and the distributions ship Stage 1 and Stage 2 from the same aura build. Existing
installations have to replace Stage 1, which needs macOS. The installer's Stage 1 update does that
without reinstalling: it enrols the new Stage 1 for the existing Linux system and keeps that
system's Stage 2 and data. Any Stage 2 from 1.0.0b11 or later runs under this Stage 1. On the M4
Pro and M4 Max, a Stage 2 from 1.0.0b13.1 or earlier doesn't adopt a live session: it powers ANS
off and starts it again. That works on T6040 and, as before this release, not reliably on T6041.

The 1.0.0b13 notes, and the 1.0.0b7 notes on the CTXR window, apply unchanged.
