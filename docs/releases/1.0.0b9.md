# Aura 1.0.0b9

Released from `main` 32580c94. Changes since 1.0.0b8 (30e93e89):

- **M4 GPU handoff on every M4 Mac (#106).** The G16 handoff is published on any T8132 Mac running OS firmware 26.6.2 (`/chosen/asahi,os-fw-version`), not only the J713. The J713 is unchanged on every firmware.
- **M4 internal display handoff on every M4 Mac with a panel (#110).** `dt_set_m4_dcp_handoff()` hands the internal DCP over on the J713 as before, or on any T8132 with an internal panel (`/arm-io/disp0` without `external`) on 26.6.2. The J715 gets the same handoff as the J713, including PIODMA and scanout. The J604 and the iMacs get the firmware UUID, version and reservations; PIODMA follows their device trees. The J773g, whose `disp0` is external, gets nothing.

The 1.0.0b7 notes, including the CTXR window requirements on M4 and later, apply unchanged.

## Qualification

- CI build (gcc), stage1-clang, format-check and pytest are green on #106's and #110's heads.
- The J713 handoff is byte-identical to 1.0.0b8 across 1428 compared cases.
- Not yet booted on the J604, J623, J624, J715 or J773g.
